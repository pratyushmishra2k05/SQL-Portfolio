# 🗃️ SQL Portfolio — Data Analysis Projects

Welcome to my **SQL Portfolio**! This repository showcases SQL projects focused on data analysis, customer behavior, sales performance, and revenue insights using relational databases and business-oriented queries.

## 📌 Projects

### 01. E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

**Objective:** Analyze e-commerce sales data to understand revenue performance, product demand, and customer purchasing behavior.

**Key Analysis Areas:**
- Sales and revenue analysis
- Product performance
- Customer purchasing patterns
- Country-wise sales analysis
- Data querying and aggregation
- Business insights from transactional data

**Skills Demonstrated:** SQL querying, filtering, aggregation, grouping, and business analysis.

**Project Folder:** `01-Ecommerce-Sales/`

---

### 02. Music Store Customer & Revenue Analysis

**Objective:** Analyze music store data to identify top customers, popular music genres, artist performance, and revenue trends.

**Key Analysis Areas:**
- Customer spending analysis
- Revenue by country and city
- Popular music genres and artists
- Invoice and sales analysis
- Customer purchase patterns
- Multi-table relational analysis

**Skills Demonstrated:** SQL JOINs, aggregate functions, subqueries, grouping, and analytical queries.

**Project Folder:** `02-Music-Store/`
